### ALGO-000237: Strong matroid secretary conjecture
- **Subfield:** Online algorithms and streaming
- **Open:** Obtain a \(1/e\)-competitive secretary algorithm for every matroid in the specified random-arrival model.
- **Status:** The constant-competitive matroid secretary conjecture was reported solved in September 2026. The stronger \(1/e\) target for all matroids remains open; a separate 2026 preprint reports \(1/e\) for linear matroids.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Seeks the optimal classical secretary constant under general independence constraints.
- **Key works:** Singla (2026), *The Matroid Secretary Conjecture Is True*, arXiv:2609.14555; Abdi, Banihashem, Hajiaghayi, and Mittal (2026), *On the Strong Matroid Secretary Conjecture and Beyond*, arXiv:2609.19118.
